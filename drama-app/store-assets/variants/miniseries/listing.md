## en-US

**App name (17/30):** MiniSeries Player

**Short description (76/80):** Your private short-series library. Add links, play vertically. No data kept.

**Full description (887/4000):**
MiniSeries Player is a simple vertical player for short dramas. It plays videos from links you add yourself. No ads, no sign-up, and no personal data collected.

Features
• Full-screen vertical player: swipe up and down to change episodes
• Autoplays the next episode when one ends
• Remembers your series and where you stopped, so you can resume instantly
• Your own library: add a series by pasting video links (.mp4 or .m3u8), one link per episode
• Shortcuts to other short-drama services when you want them

Privacy
• No accounts, no ads, no analytics
• Your series list and progress stay on your device
• Requests Internet permission only, to play video

Note
This app is a player and does not include any videos or content. Use only links to content you have the right to play. Video links must use https.
Privacy policy: https://envburiram.github.io/drama/privacy-miniseries.html
